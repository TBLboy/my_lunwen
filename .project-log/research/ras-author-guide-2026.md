# RAS Author Guide

- Source: `https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/robotics-and-autonomous-systems/publish/guide-for-authors`
- Captured: 2026-09-17

## Confirmed Submission Constraints

- The journal accepts editable LaTeX source files and permits double-column formatting for LaTeX submissions.
- The title page must include the article title, full author names, full affiliations with postal addresses and country, and one corresponding author with email.
- The abstract must be concise and no longer than 250 words.
- Keywords: 1 to 7 English keywords; multiword phrases using "and" or "of" should be avoided where possible.
- Highlights: 3 to 5 bullets, each no more than 85 characters including spaces.
- Highlights must be supplied as a separate editable file with "highlights" in the filename.
- Tables must be editable text, cited in text, numbered in order, and supplied with captions.
- Figures must be cited in text, numbered in order, and supplied as separate files.
- Each figure requires a caption with a brief title and description.
- Vector artwork is preferred in EPS or PDF.
- CRediT contributor roles should be provided.
- Acknowledgements, if any, belong in a separate section immediately before the references.
- A data statement and data availability information are required when applicable.
- A generative AI declaration is required when AI tools were used in manuscript preparation.
- References should use numbered square-bracket citations and be numbered in order of first appearance.
- The guide does not state an eight-page limit for this journal.
